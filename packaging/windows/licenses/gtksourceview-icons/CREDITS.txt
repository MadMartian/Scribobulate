This credit is not decoration. CC BY-SA 3.0 s4(c) requires the attribution to be
given; the licence text alone does not discharge it, so this file ships beside
CC-BY-SA-3.0.txt rather than in place of it.

The 15 files it covers are share\icons\hicolor\scalable\actions\*.svg in the
installed tree: lang-class, lang-define, lang-enum, lang-enum-value,
lang-function, lang-include, lang-method, lang-namespace, lang-struct,
lang-struct-field, lang-typedef, lang-union, lang-variable,
completion-snippet and completion-word, each -symbolic.

They are redistributed UNMODIFIED. That matters: ShareAlike leaves us with
nothing further to do only while that stays true, and recolouring one of these
SVGs to match a theme would make an Adaptation and bring s4(b) into play.

WHY THIS TEXT IS VENDORED AND NOT COPIED FROM THE GTK PREFIX. It cannot come
from the prefix. Upstream's data/icons/meson.build installs the icon tree with
install_subdir('hicolor', ...) and installs no COPYING beside it, so the file
that governs these icons -- GtkSourceView's own data/icons/COPYING, which reads
"The icons here are licensed under the CC-by-SA 3." -- has never existed in any
installed tree, in any prefix, on any platform.

That absence is the whole reason this row was wrong twice. The icons sit in an
installed tree with no licence file next to them and no per-file header, which
reads like an absence of any special terms and is in fact an absence of the
DECLARATION, not of the terms. The root COPYING is LGPL-2.1 and governs the
code; it does not reach the artwork.
