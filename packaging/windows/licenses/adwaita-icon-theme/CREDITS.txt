ELECTION
--------

adwaita-icon-theme is offered under a choice of two licences. Its COPYING
reads:

    "This work is licenced under the terms of either the GNU LGPL v3 or
     Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 3.0 United States License."

This product elects CC-BY-SA-3.0. The icons are redistributed unmodified, so
no Adaptation is created and the ShareAlike limb of section 4(b) is not
engaged. Section 4(a) explicitly does not subject the surrounding Collection
to this licence, so the Apache-2.0 licence of Scribobulate itself is
unaffected.

The election is this redistributor's to make and is recorded rather than
implied: the GTK prefix installs only COPYING_CCBYSA3, and allowing "whichever
text upstream happened to install" to decide the question is how a licence
gets chosen by accident.
